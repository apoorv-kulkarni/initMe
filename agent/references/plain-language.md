# Plain Language

Write RFCs, Confluence pages, PRs, and messages for a broad technical
audience. Assume readers may be new to the topic. Use clear, common words
and explain technical terms when needed. Keep the technical meaning accurate
so readers can understand and learn.

- Lead with the result, problem, or needed action.
- Use common words. Prefer "use" over "utilize" and "source" or "history"
  over "provenance."
- Keep sentences short. Each sentence should express one main idea.
- Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences.
- Avoid idioms, metaphors, formal wording, and uncommon literary words.
- Define acronyms and technical terms the first time they appear.
- Use technical terms only when they provide needed accuracy.
- Do not remove important technical details. Explain them in simpler words.
- Remove filler, repeated points, and background that does not help the
  reader act.
- Before finishing, reread the text and simplify anything a reader who is
  new to the topic may not understand on the first read.
