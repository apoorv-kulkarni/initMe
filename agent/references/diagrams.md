# Diagrams

Use diagrams when they make an idea easier to understand.

- Prefer a diagram over a long explanation of a flow, sequence, system,
  dependency, or before-and-after change.
- Use Mermaid for Markdown, RFCs, and PRs.
- Keep diagrams small and use plain labels.
- Split large diagrams into smaller focused diagrams.
- Add a short text summary so the diagram is not the only explanation.
- Skip diagrams for simple facts or one-step actions.
